# Safety & High-Stress UI Principles

1. **One-Handed Use**: Essential actions (SOS, Check-in, Call Guardian) MUST be reachable with a thumb while holding the phone in one hand. Place these in the bottom 30% of the screen.
2. **Unambiguous Colors**: Red always means danger or immediate action. Green means safe. Do not use semantic colors for decorative purposes.
3. **Frictionless Escapes**: Emergency actions must require minimal cognitive load. Use large touch targets (minimum 48x48px) and predictable placement.
4. **Offline Resilience**: Safety UI should communicate clearly even when offline, gracefully degrading without showing raw technical errors.
